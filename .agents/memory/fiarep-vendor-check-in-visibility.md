description: Authority boundary for vendor walkthrough check-in timestamps and location.

Vendor walkthrough check-in timestamps and location details are operational data for Procurement only. The vendor may see confirmation of their own submission, but other staff roles must not receive or display the check-in details.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed that vendor timestamps go to Procurement only.

**How to apply:** Keep notifications targeted to Procurement, filter check-in details from records returned to other staff roles, and preserve the vendor's own submission confirmation.
