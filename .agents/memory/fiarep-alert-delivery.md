---
name: FIAREP alert delivery
description: Required delivery and presentation behavior for operational alerts.
---

Resident complaints go immediately to the operational supervisors assigned to the complaint's development. Superintendent Ⓔ always receives them as the emergency supervisor, regardless of development assignment. Upper management may read complaints but must not assign, update, approve, clear, or delete them. Notification listing, unread counts, sync, read actions, and push recipients must use the same report authorization boundary. When a supervisor assigns operational work, the server must target the assigned worker's stable staff ID with durable notification and native push delivery; workers use My Jobs as the only in-app assignment queue, with its button showing the active job count instead of a separate Inbox. Emergency, priority, elevator-down, and new Resident-report notifications must remain in the management Inbox and appear red immediately; opening the Inbox must never delete emergency notifications.

Only Borough Directors and Regional Directors may mute alert interruptions. Regular Management, Property Managers, Superintendents, and other supervisors must not see the mute control. Muting suppresses sound, vibration, and background push on that device, but red visual alerts and Inbox records remain available.

Human Resources pending-employee reminders must open the exact employee record, remain available until approval, and preserve HR's saved notes about missing documents or unfinished onboarding work.

**Why:** Complaint handling belongs to development supervision, while upper management oversight is read-only. Role-wide alerts paired with development-scoped records disclose complaints to the wrong staff and create alerts that authorized recipients cannot open. Inbox-only database inserts, discarded sync notifications, delayed age-based escalation, emergency cleanup, and HR reminders that lose their employee context can silently hide unfinished work.

**How to apply:** Target resident-report notifications to stable IDs for development-assigned operational supervisors plus Superintendent Ⓔ. Keep upper management read authorization separate from all complaint mutation authority. Fail closed when a legacy role-targeted alert references a missing or unreadable report. Every server-created operational notification must invoke push delivery. Route worker assignment taps to My Jobs and compute its visible count from active jobs assigned to that stable staff ID; do not expose a separate Worker Inbox button. Every synced notification must be persisted locally and produce sound/badge feedback when newly received unless that upper manager has muted the device. Restrict mute UI and behavior to Borough Director and Regional Director positions; unregister muted devices from background push and re-register when unmuted. Retry device-token registration after login and session restoration. Expo Go cannot provide production remote push delivery, so retain the local synced-alert fallback; use an installed native build for background remote push.
