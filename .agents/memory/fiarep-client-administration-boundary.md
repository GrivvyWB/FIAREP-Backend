---
name: FIAREP client administration boundary
description: Defines when Platform Control authority ends and client staff administration begins.
---

Platform Control creates the client organization and its first Borough Director. After that initial handoff, Platform Control must not add, replace, reset, or delete the client's directors or other staff. Borough Director succession and all later staff administration happen inside the client's Team Directory.

**Why:** The platform administrator licenses and configures the client but does not remain the client's personnel administrator. The client must control its own leadership succession after activation.

**How to apply:** Keep initial Borough Director creation in organization onboarding. Put replacement safeguards and staff-account actions in tenant-authenticated Team flows, and do not expose post-onboarding staff controls through platform-owner routes or screens.
