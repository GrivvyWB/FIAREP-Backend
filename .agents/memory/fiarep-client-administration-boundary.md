---
name: FIAREP client administration boundary
description: Defines when Platform Control authority ends and client staff administration begins.
---

Platform Control creates the client organization and its first Borough Director. After that initial handoff, Platform Control must not add, replace, reset, or delete the client's directors or other staff. Inside the client, the first Borough Director directly creates the first Human Resources account because no HR Workspace exists yet. After HR exists, HR handles ordinary employee intake, while Borough Director succession remains in the Team Directory.

**Why:** The platform administrator licenses and configures the client but does not remain the client's personnel administrator. The client must control its own leadership succession after activation.

**How to apply:** Keep initial Borough Director creation in organization onboarding. Allow the first Borough Director to bootstrap exactly one active HR account and add a replacement Borough Director. Route later ordinary employee intake through HR, and do not expose post-onboarding staff controls through platform-owner routes or screens.
