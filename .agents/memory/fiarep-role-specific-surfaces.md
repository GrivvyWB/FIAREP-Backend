---
name: FIAREP role-specific surfaces
description: Keeps field and supervisory roles on separate web and mobile navigation surfaces.
---

Inspector, Supervisor Inspector, CPM, CPM Supervisor, each Trade Supervisor, and each Trade Worker must have separate role-specific tabs and guarded routes. Handoffs connect records between roles; they do not expose another role's navigation or controls. Trade Workers need their own assigned-work surface with start, real photo evidence, and completion.

Team-directory placement must follow the employee's current saved role and position. Do not permanently pin individual account IDs to HR or another group; a reassignment must move the employee after reload.

**Why:** Shared management or inspector menus expose unrelated controls and blur accountability at each required handoff. Hard-coded HR membership caused reassigned employees to snap back to HR even after their assignment changed.

**How to apply:** Build visible navigation and directory groups from exact current role-and-position pairs, guard critical pages at the route/screen boundary, and retain server authorization for every read and action. Higher management, HR, and Procurement keep their own established surfaces.
