NYCHA Development Maps display residential and non-residential structures with building and address numbers, as well as tax lot boundaries. They include information on the NYCHA building and stairhall numbers, NYC Department of Building Identification Numbers (BIN), tax block and lot numbers, and AMP (Asset Management Project) numbers, and any facilities located at each address number.
