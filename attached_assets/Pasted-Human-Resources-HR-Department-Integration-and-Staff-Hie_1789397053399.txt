Human Resources (HR) Department Integration and Staff Hierarchy Requirements
Overview

Add a Human Resources (HR) Department to the application. HR will be responsible for creating, managing, and assigning all staff members throughout the organization.

The HR module will serve as the central location where employees are added, edited, transferred, and assigned to developments, regions, and supervisors.

HR Employee Assignment Rules
Supervisor-Based Assignments

When HR creates an employee, the employee must automatically be assigned under the appropriate supervisor.

Examples:

If a Plumber is added, assign them under the Plumbing Supervisor.
If an Electrician is added, assign them under the Electrical Supervisor.
If a Maintenance Worker is added, assign them under the Maintenance Supervisor.
If a Caretaker is added, assign them to the Superintendent.
If an Inspector is added, assign them to the designated Inspector Supervisor or Superintendent.

The employee hierarchy should be visible in a reporting structure.

Example:

Regional Director
 └── Property Manager
      └── Superintendent
           ├── Assistant Superintendent
           ├── Plumbing Supervisor
           │    ├── Plumber
           │    ├── Plumber
           │    └── Plumber
           ├── Electrical Supervisor
           │    ├── Electrician
           │    └── Electrician
           ├── Maintenance Supervisor
           │    ├── Maintenance Worker
           │    └── Maintenance Worker
           └── Caretakers

Development Assignment Rules

Certain management staff must be assigned to specific developments before they become active within that development.

The following positions are development-specific:

Regional Director
Assistant Regional Director
Property Manager
Superintendent
Assistant Superintendent

When HR creates any of these positions:

The user must select a development.
The employee will only appear under that assigned development.
One employee may be assigned to multiple developments if permitted by business rules.

Example:

Development A
 ├── Property Manager
 ├── Superintendent
 └── Assistant Superintendent

Development B
 ├── Property Manager
 ├── Superintendent
 └── Assistant Superintendent

Supervisor Coverage Rules

Supervisors may:

Be assigned to a single development.
Be assigned to multiple developments.
Be assigned globally across all developments.

If no specific development is selected, the supervisor should be considered available organization-wide.

Caretaker Management Rule

Caretakers report directly to the Superintendent.

Example:

Superintendent
 ├── Caretaker #1
 ├── Caretaker #2
 ├── Caretaker #3


The Superintendent must have the ability to:

View all assigned caretakers.
Create work assignments.
Track caretaker activities.
Reassign caretakers if needed.

Core Logic

The HR Department is the master source for all staffing records.

Whenever a new employee is added:

HR selects the employee position.
The system determines the proper supervisor.
The system determines whether the position requires a development assignment.
The employee is automatically placed into the organizational hierarchy.
The employee appears under the appropriate supervisor and development.

This structure should support all current and future staff positions while maintaining a clear reporting hierarchy across regions, developments, supervisors, and field employees.
